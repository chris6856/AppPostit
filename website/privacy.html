Privacy Policy

In short: we don't collect anything.

Last updated: July 28, 2026

AppPostIt ("the app") lets you save reusable text ("posts"), organize them into categories, and insert them into other apps using a custom keyboard. This policy explains what data the app collects. In short: none.

Data collection

AppPostIt does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising SDK. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Where your data lives

The categories and posts you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, because AppPostIt does not have one. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted along with it.

The AppPostIt keyboard

AppPostIt includes an optional custom keyboard that you can enable in your device's settings. Like any keyboard app, enabling it grants it the technical ability to see what you type, which is why Android shows a warning before you turn it on. AppPostIt's keyboard only reads the posts you've saved in the app and inserts the one you tap — it does not record, transmit, or store anything you type elsewhere.

Links to other services

The app's "How-to videos" option opens AppPostIt's YouTube channel (youtube.com/@AppPostit) in an in-app browser view. Google/YouTube's own privacy policy applies to your interaction with that content; AppPostIt does not receive any information from it.

Children's privacy

AppPostIt does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data collection), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@apppostit.com.
