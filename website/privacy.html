Privacy Policy

In short: we don't collect anything, and Smart Reply only sends what you ask it to.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

AppPostIt ("the app") lets you save reusable text ("posts"), organize them into categories, and insert them into other apps using a custom keyboard. This policy explains what data the app collects. In short: none. The optional Smart Reply feature sends text to our assistant only when you ask it for a reply, as described below.

Data collection

AppPostIt does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising SDK. Apart from Smart Reply (below) and app store purchases, the app makes no network requests of its own.

Where your data lives

The categories and posts you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, because AppPostIt does not have one. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted along with it.

Smart Reply

Smart Reply's assistant drafts a reply to a message you paste into it. It works only when you tap Ask Our Assistant, Shorten or Regenerate, and asks for your permission the first time. Each time, the app sends AppPostIt's server the message you pasted, the response profile and tone you picked, any saved posts you chose as reference, and a random ID created by the app (not linked to you or your device) used only to enforce daily usage limits.

Our assistant is powered by Claude, an AI service provided by Anthropic: AppPostIt's server passes this text to Claude to write the reply, and returns the reply to the app. AppPostIt does not store or log the text you send. To enforce daily limits, the server keeps a count of requests per hashed app ID and hashed network address, deleted after a day or two. Anthropic processes the text under its privacy policy and commercial terms, which do not allow using it to train its models, and may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. Please don't paste passwords, payment details or other sensitive information into Smart Reply.

The AppPostIt keyboard

AppPostIt includes an optional custom keyboard that you can enable in your device's settings. Like any keyboard app, enabling it grants it the technical ability to see what you type, which is why Android shows a warning before you turn it on. AppPostIt's keyboard only reads the posts you've saved in the app and inserts the one you tap — it does not record, transmit, or store anything you type elsewhere.

Links to other services

The app's "How-to videos" option opens AppPostIt's YouTube channel (youtube.com/@AppPostit) in an in-app browser view. Google/YouTube's own privacy policy applies to your interaction with that content; AppPostIt does not receive any information from it.

Children's privacy

AppPostIt does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Smart Reply only sends text that the user pastes in and chooses to send, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data collection), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
