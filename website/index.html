How it works

Four taps to your next reply

Set it up once, then reuse it everywhere you type.

1

Build your categories

Create categories for the replies you give most — like "Allergies," "Product Questions," or "Thank Yous."

2

Save your posts

Add the exact text you want to reuse inside each category, with an optional short label.

3

Switch keyboards

While typing anywhere, switch to the AppPostIt keyboard — same as switching to an emoji keyboard.

4

Tap to insert

Pick a category, tap a post, and it's inserted instantly. Switch back and keep going.

New

Got a message? Get a reply.

Paste in an email, a Facebook comment, a Reddit post, a support ticket or a text message, and Smart Reply's assistant drafts a reply for you in seconds.

  • Pick a response profile — General, Customer Support, Technical Support, Sales or Social Media, or create your own.
  • Choose a tone — Helpful, Professional or Casual.
  • Use your saved posts as reference, so replies include your own facts, links and wording.
  • Edit, shorten or regenerate, then copy the reply or save it as a post for the keyboard.

Your first 3 Smart Replies are free. After that, reply packs of 50 ($1.99) or 125 ($4.99) are available in the app and never expire. Prices shown in US dollars.
