How it works

Four taps to your next reply

Set it up once, then reuse it everywhere you type.

1

Build your categories

Create categories for the replies you give most — like "Allergies," "Product Questions," or "Thank Yous."

2

Save your posts

Add the exact text you want to reuse inside each category, with an optional short label.

3

Switch keyboards

While typing anywhere, switch to the AppPostIt keyboard — same as switching to an emoji keyboard.

4

Tap to insert

Pick a category, tap a post, and it's inserted instantly. Switch back and keep going.
